Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 6, 2026

BevGolf is better with friends: your posts, notes, comments and photos show up in other players' feeds. These guidelines keep it fun for everyone. They're part of our Terms & Conditions, so using BevGolf means agreeing to them.

Trash talk is fine. Going after people isn't.

Ribbing a friend about a three-putt is part of the game, and so is the odd swear word. What isn't allowed is anything meant to hurt, scare or degrade someone.

Not allowed

  • Hate speech and slurs. No attacking or demeaning anyone for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  • Harassment and bullying. No threats, no targeting a player, no tagging or commenting on someone who's made clear they don't want it, and no sharing anyone's private information.
  • Sexual content and nudity. No explicit language, sexual images or nudity.
  • Graphic violence. No gore, and nothing that celebrates violence or self-harm.
  • Dangerous or illegal activity. No promoting illegal activity, drinking and driving, or underage drinking, and no pressuring anyone to drink more than they want to.
  • Spam and scams. No advertising, no repeated posts, and no links meant to trick people or take their login details.
  • Impersonation. Don't pretend to be another player, a public figure or BevGolf.

These rules cover everything other players can see: posts, notes, comments, photos, usernames, full names, guest names and beverage names. Beverage names are shared with every player who searches for one, so pick them like you'd pick a username.

How we keep it that way

  • Text is checked before it's posted. Posts, comments, names and beverage names are checked against a list of blocked words. If something is blocked, you'll see "Message contains inappropriate content" and it won't be posted.
  • Blocked attempts are logged and may be reviewed.
  • Getting around the checks counts as breaking the rules. That includes misspellings, swapped letters and spacing tricks.
  • We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive players. We may remove content, and suspend or close accounts, without warning. Serious breaches, like slurs or threats, and repeated ones lead to losing your account.

See something? Report it

Tap ••• on any post, comment or profile and choose Report. That opens an email to support@bevgolf.app with what you're reporting already filled in. We review every report, and aim to act within 24 hours.

Play responsibly

BevGolf works with any beverage. If you choose to drink alcohol, follow the laws where you are, know your limits and never drink and drive.

Questions or think we got it wrong?

Email support@bevgolf.app. If we've removed something of yours or blocked a name you think is fine, tell us and we'll take another look.
