What is Bev Golf

A real golf game. Your glass is the swing.

Every hole plays like golf: clubs, wind, bunkers, water and trees. What's different is how you hit the ball. Each shot gives you a target on your glass, and how close your sip lands decides where the ball goes.

Play the course

Pick a club and a high, standard or low flight. Wind, bunkers, rough, fescue, water and trees all change where the ball ends up, so every shot is a decision.

Drink to hit

Take one unbroken sip and stop on the line. A perfect drink lands the ball right on your aim point. Miss the line and the ball sprays, sometimes by a lot.

How to play

Pour. Par. Repeat.

All you need is an iPhone and a drink you can see through. Here's a hole, start to finish.

Step 1

Prep

Grab a clear glass, bottle or container. A pint glass works best. Fill it with whatever you like. If you're drinking alcohol, think about alternating with something non-alcoholic as you play.

Step 2

Pick your shot

Choose a club and a flight, and set your aim point. It shows what a perfect drink does: the ball lands right in the crosshairs, then rolls out.

Step 3

Drink your shot

Tap Play Shot, stand your phone next to your glass and mark where your drink starts. Your target appears: take one unbroken sip and try to stop right on it. Put the glass down, drag the marker to where you finished, and submit to see where the ball goes.

Step 4

Play it out

Keep going until you've holed out on every hole, then post your card. To keep things fair there's a minimum level in the glass: when you run low, refill or swap drinks before you play on.

Every lie plays differently

Bunkers, rough and fescue cost you distance and roll. Play from a bad lie and you'll need a more accurate drink to get out of trouble. Trees can deflect the ball, and the game shows how likely your line is to clip one.

  • High More wind, less roll
  • Standard Some wind, some roll
  • Low Least wind, most roll

Ways to play

Solo, head to head, or chasing the pot.

Play a quick hole on your own, take a friend on, or line up your one swing of the day in the Ace Race.

Solo

Play a single hole or a full course from the front or back tees. Back tees ask for bigger sips; front tees give you a smaller target.

Head to Head

Challenge a friend and take turns, passing one phone or each on your own, and watch their shots land as they play.

Ace Race

One hole, one swing a day. Get closest to the pin to top the board. Every miss grows the pot of Tabs, the in-game currency, and a hole-in-one takes it.

Drink Only

No golf, just the glass. Single Shot, Triple Shot and Bubble Pop test how well you can stop exactly where you mean to.

Play responsibly

Any drink works.

Water, soda, juice, iced coffee, a beer: Bev Golf measures how far down the glass you drink, not what's in it. What you pour is up to you.

If you choose to drink alcohol, follow the laws where you are, know your limits and never drink and drive.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I play?

Bev Golf is coming soon to the App Store. Leave your email and we'll tell you the day it's out.

Do I have to drink alcohol?

No. Bev Golf works with any drink, water included. The game only cares how far down the glass your sip goes.

What do I need?

An iPhone and a drink in something clear, so you can see the level. A pint glass is ideal. Your phone stands next to the glass while you take each shot.

Is it on Android?

Not at launch. Bev Golf is starting on iPhone.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Challenge a friend head to head on one phone or two, follow each other's rounds in the feed, and compete in the Ace Race.

Why can't I play some courses?

State courses open only while you're in their state: Maple Country Club in Vermont, Northern Links in Maine, Ocean State in Rhode Island, Old Colony in Massachusetts, Purple Finch in New Hampshire and The Orchard in New York. BGA Links and the Water Park are open everywhere.
